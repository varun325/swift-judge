Implement `struct TwoStackQueue<Element>` with `mutating func enqueue(_:)`, `mutating func dequeue() -> Element?`, `var peek: Element?` (non-mutating!) and `var count`, using two arrays as stacks with **amortised O(1)** operations.

 The judge drives it with ops `enqueue <n>` / `dequeue` / `peek` / `count`.
