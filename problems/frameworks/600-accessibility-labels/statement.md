Swiftful's *Accessibility: Voice Over*. A product row visually shows `★★★★☆ 4.2`, `$19.99` and a `🔥` badge — VoiceOver would read that as gibberish. Build a spoken label: `"<name>, <price> dollars, rated <rating> out of 5 stars<, bestseller>"`. Rows are `[name, priceCents, rating, isBestseller "y"/"n"]`; ratings render with one decimal; prices say `"<dollars> dollars <cents> cents"` (omit cents when 0, singular `dollar`/`cent` for 1).
