HealthKit returns step samples as intervals; charts need **per-day totals in the user's time zone**. Each sample is `[startISO, count]`. Group by calendar day in the given zone (not UTC!), sum counts, and fill missing days between the first and last with 0. Return `"yyyy-MM-dd <total>"` lines. This is what `HKStatisticsCollectionQuery` does with an anchor date and a 1-day interval.
