L9 tracks how long a game has been played, pausing when the app leaves the screen. Store `elapsedBeforePause: TimeInterval` and `startTime: Date?` (non-nil while running). Events are `[action, secondsSinceReference]` with actions `start`, `pause`, `check`. `elapsed(at:)` = stored time + (now − start) when running. Log `check` results as `"m:ss"` (what `Text(.offset)`-style formatting shows).
