Apps should persist work when the scene leaves the foreground. `@Environment(\.scenePhase)` moves between `active`, `inactive` and `background`. Given the phase sequence and, for each step, how many unsaved edits were made just before it, log `"save <n>"` when transitioning **to `background`** with pending edits (then reset), `"refresh"` when returning to `active` **from `background`**, and `"-"` otherwise. Ignore repeated identical phases.
